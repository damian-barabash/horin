Deformed
01 / Collection

In concrete

Tailoring cast under pressure. Shoulders are poured like formwork, waists fracture along the stress lines, hems carry the weight of the slab. The collection begins where the body meets the wall — and refuses to come out unchanged.

Form
02 / Silhouette

Under pressure

Cropped blazers with cantilevered shoulders. Trousers that flare like cracks running to the ground. Every look is a load-bearing structure: strict on the surface, deformed at the core.

Raw
03 / Craft

Surface & seam

Dense wool, dry cotton, hardware stripped to zero. Seams stay exposed like rebar — the garment admits how it was built. Nothing decorative survives the pour.
